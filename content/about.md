Programming has been part of my life for quite a while, from writing Pawn scripts for SA:MP to the present day. In recent years, low-level programming has become my main interest. After spending a long time thinking about starting a blog to share my projects and ideas, I’ve finally created this space to explore topics I’ve wanted to write about, develop ideas that never made it beyond drafts, and share whatever new ideas come to mind. I’m also curious to see where this whole LLM era will lead us.

Hope you enjoy it!
